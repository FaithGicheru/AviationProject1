# AviationProject1
## Background 
This project involves analyzing accident data from the aviation industry to identify the aircraft with the lowest operational risk for both private and commercial operations.
### Business Understanding
The company wants to delve into aviation and seeks to minimize operational risks. This analysis will help the Head of the Aviation Division in making an informed decision regarding purchasing aircraft with the lowest operational risk.
## Key question
Which airplane has the least operational risk overall?
What is the effect of weather conditions on airplane operations?
Which aircraft make shows the lowest rate of fatal injuries?
